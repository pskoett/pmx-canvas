Design session’s recommendation — A
The human should see what the agent is told The brief already carries “review due: check the source before relying on it” on Churn by month. In B that card looks clean on the board while the agent is told to doubt it, so the human and the agent are looking at two different boards. That is the same gap the pin and the “changed” mark were designed to close. A costs one quiet line, on only the cards that are due — 2 of the 4 here, and on a real board usually a small share. Everything else (rates, clocks, history, inheritance, unknown) still goes into the menu, which keeps the maintainer’s “not on the board” for everything that isn’t a call to act. If B is chosen, keep one tie to the card: the count popover lists the cards and jumps to them, and the brief panel names them, so the mismatch is at least one click away. Either way the Lifecycle board’s at-rest card lines are replaced by the chosen variant once decided.
