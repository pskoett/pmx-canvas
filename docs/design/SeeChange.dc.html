“See change” in the session receipt

When a session ends, its receipt lists what the agent edited. “See change” opens the change in place: Before above After, the changed words marked, and Undo for that card alone. Stacked, not side by side — the receipt is 290 px wide, and two columns would leave each about 118 px. Violet marks what the agent wrote; nothing here is amber unless it needs a look.
