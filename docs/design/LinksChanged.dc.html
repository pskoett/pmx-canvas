When a card’s links change after the agent read it

Connecting cards is the work of a research board, so it must not paint the board amber. Proposed: the mark sits on what changed — the edge — not on both cards. A links-only change is not “needs a look”: the next brief resends both cards with their new relations by itself. Amber stays for text the agent may be acting on.
